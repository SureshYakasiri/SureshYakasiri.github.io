// 01. PROFILE OVERVIEW

ENGINEERING GAMEPLAY THROUGH DESIGN RIGOR

I am a Game Design student at ICAT Design & Media College, pursuing a B.Sc in Game Art & Design with a major in Game Designing (Expected Graduation: 2027).

My design philosophy centers on turning creative ideas into playable experiences. I enjoy dissecting and architecting gameplay mechanics, constructing balanced level progressions, experimenting with bold concepts, and shaping the exact moment-to-moment feedback players feel with controller or keyboard in hand.

Through competitive game jams like BYOG and Brackeys, I have honed the discipline of rapid prototyping under tight constraints—iterating rapidly from core concept documentation to fully functional Unity greyboxes and release-ready builds.
